# Provider icons

The icons of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, Ollama, DeepSeek, OpenRouter and LM Studio are from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0). The names and marks belong to their owners; Totem shows them only to identify the provider that a person has chosen. The icons of Groq, llama.cpp and Together AI were added by hand later; their marks belong to their owners and are used in the same way. A provider without an icon shows its initial.
