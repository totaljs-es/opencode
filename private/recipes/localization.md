Every text for people is written in English between `@(` and `)`: in views, in plugins and in actions. The translations are in `resources/<language>.resource`.

```js
$.invalid('@(The ticket does not exist)');
```

```html
<button class="exec" data-exec="?/save">@(Save)</button>
```
